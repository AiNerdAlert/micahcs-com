How it starts

One conversation first.

Matt meets every student before they enroll, so you get the right option and not a generic package.

  1. ApplyTell Matt who the lessons are for and what you want to do.
  2. Matt repliesHe reviews every application, then sends a few times, usually within two or three days.
  3. ConsultationMatt hears where you are, finds what's holding you back, and recommends the right option.

MLA may not be right if

  • You want a single lesson or a cheap trial.
  • You want to start without talking to Matt first.
  • You don't want to practice or play with other people.

For Matt and Micah

Why the page works this way

  • The headline sells the result people want, playing with other musicians, before it lists any products.
  • The three options are equal-sized peers, so a visitor picks the one that sounds like them and never feels pushed.
  • Each card opens with "Best for" in the visitor's own situation, since people choose by recognizing themselves.
  • The included value sits next to each item, so $277 reads as a full membership instead of four lessons.
  • Studio class only costs more than its included value on purpose. It shows what the bundle is worth.
  • There is one next step, a consultation, because Matt wants to meet every student before enrollment.

Five things to confirm

  1. Do studio-class-only members get Live Room Sessions, or are they only for private students?
  2. Can teens join the studio class on its own, or only after private lessons?
  3. Are "Private coaching with Matt", "Online Studio Class", and "Live Room Sessions" the names we want in public?
  4. How far away can online students live? The page needs one plain sentence.
  5. Is the $247 standalone price right, and does it include a setup fee?
