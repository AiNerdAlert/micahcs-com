Where does a new visitor start?

Music Lessons Arizona teaches guitar, bass, and drums to adults and teens, in person in Tempe or online. Every student gets private coaching, plus an online studio class and live playing sessions, so they learn to play with other musicians and not only practice alone.

6

Tell Matt about you

Not a choice. This is what Matt needs to read before he decides whether to offer a consultation.

Must have

  • Name, email, and phone
  • Who the lessons are for. For a child: name, age, and parent contact
  • Instrument
  • Where they live, and in person or online
  • Level, in their own words
  • What they want to be able to do
  • What feels stuck right now

Nice to have

  • How many hours a week they can practice
  • Past teachers or lessons
  • Their instrument and gear
  • Best days and times for lessons
  • Whether they already play with other people
  • How they heard about MLA

Six things to confirm

Once these are answered, we can turn this path into the homepage and the application form.

  1. Is the beginner rule right? Beginners work with other teachers, intermediate and advanced players work with Matt.
  2. Which teacher covers which instrument? Does Matt teach bass or drums directly?
  3. How far can online students live? Nationwide, worldwide, or certain time zones?
  4. Should a parent complete the application for a child, or a parent and child together?
  5. Is the studio class available to children and teens, or only after the foundation is built?
  6. Is the must-have list right for the application form, or is it too long?
