How the offers connect

One entry point. A recommended path.

People should not have to compare every program before contacting MLA. Matt assesses fit, then recommends the right starting point.

  1. 01Apply

    Tell MLA about the student, their playing, and what they want.

  2. 02Meet Matt

    Use the assessment to identify the main issue and confirm fit.

  3. 03Start in the right program

    Private coaching, Matt's program, or a suitable studio class.

  4. 04Grow into real playing

    Add studio classes or Live Room sessions when the student is ready.

Shared commitment described in the interview

Programs begin with a two-month commitment paid up front, then move to month-to-month automatic billing. Matt needs to confirm whether this applies to every offer.

Useful distinction

Important, but not separate offers

The current website makes teaching topics, formats, and programs feel like separate products. This simpler grouping should be easier to understand.

Application and assessment

This is the entry step that helps Matt diagnose the problem and recommend the right program. The 360 page currently presents a complimentary 60-minute online assessment, but MLA's version still needs confirmation.

Digital resource library

Recorded classes, charts, PDFs, theory material, and lead-guitar resources support the paid program. They do not appear to be the main product.

Styles and skills

Jazz, blues, funk, rock, improvisation, rhythm, theory, songwriting, and arranging are teaching content inside the programs, not separate offers by default.
